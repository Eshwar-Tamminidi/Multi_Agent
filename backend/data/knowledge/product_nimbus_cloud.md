# Nimbus Cloud Platform – Product Overview

## What is Nimbus Cloud?
Nimbus Cloud is the company's flagship platform for real-time analytics. It ingests streaming data from IoT sensors, applications and databases, and provides dashboards, alerting and machine-learning based forecasting.

## Pricing Tiers
Nimbus Cloud is offered in three tiers. The Starter tier costs 49 USD per month and includes 5 dashboards and 10 GB of storage. The Business tier costs 299 USD per month and includes unlimited dashboards, 500 GB of storage and anomaly detection. The Enterprise tier has custom pricing and adds single sign-on, a 99.95% uptime SLA and a dedicated customer success manager.

## Service Level Agreement
The Enterprise tier guarantees 99.95% monthly uptime. If uptime falls below this target, customers receive a service credit of 10% of the monthly fee for every 0.1% below the target, up to a maximum of 50%.

## Data Retention
Raw event data is retained for 13 months on the Business tier and 36 months on the Enterprise tier. Starter tier data is retained for 90 days.

## Integrations
Nimbus Cloud integrates natively with Kafka, PostgreSQL, Snowflake, Slack, PagerDuty and Microsoft Teams. A REST API and Python SDK are available for custom integrations.
