# Nimbus Corp Information Security Policy

## Passwords and Authentication
All corporate accounts must use passwords of at least 14 characters. Passwords must be rotated every 180 days and may not reuse any of the last 5 passwords. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) using the Nimbus Authenticator app or a hardware security key is mandatory for email, VPN, source control and all production systems.

## Device Security
Company laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled and must auto-lock after 5 minutes of inactivity. Personal devices may access corporate email only after enrolling in the mobile device management (MDM) program. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT Service Desk within 1 hour.

## Data Classification
Data is classified into four levels: Public, Internal, Confidential and Restricted. Restricted data, which includes customer payment information and employee health records, must never be stored on personal devices or shared through external file-sharing services.

## Phishing and Incident Reporting
Suspicious emails must be reported using the "Report Phish" button in the mail client. Security incidents should be reported to security@nimbuscorp.example or by calling the 24x7 Security Operations Center on extension 7700.

## Access Reviews
Managers must review access rights for their team members every quarter. Access for departing employees is revoked automatically on their last working day at 18:00 local time.
