# Nimbus Corp Leave and Time-Off Policy

## Annual Leave
Full-time employees at Nimbus Corp accrue 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, credited at a rate of 2 days per month. Part-time employees accrue leave on a pro-rata basis. Up to 10 unused annual leave days may be carried forward into the next calendar year; any balance above 10 days expires on 31 March.

## Sick Leave
Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for any sick leave longer than 3 consecutive working days. Sick leave does not carry forward and cannot be encashed.

## Parental Leave
Primary caregivers are entitled to 26 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers are entitled to 6 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Parental leave must begin within 12 months of the birth or adoption of the child.

## Requesting Leave
All leave requests must be submitted through the PeopleHub portal at least 14 days in advance for planned leave of 3 days or more. Managers must approve or reject a request within 3 working days. Emergency leave can be applied for retroactively within 2 working days of returning.

## Public Holidays
Nimbus Corp observes 11 public holidays per year. The holiday calendar for each office is published on PeopleHub every December for the following year.
