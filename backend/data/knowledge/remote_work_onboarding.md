# Nimbus Corp Remote Work and Onboarding Guide

## Hybrid Work Model
Nimbus Corp follows a hybrid model: employees are expected in the office on Tuesdays and Thursdays, and may work remotely the other three days. Fully remote arrangements require approval from a department head.

## Home Office Stipend
Every employee receives a one-time home office stipend of 500 USD to purchase a desk, chair or monitor, plus a monthly internet allowance of 40 USD.

## Core Hours
Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in the employee's local time zone. Meetings should be scheduled within core hours whenever possible.

## Onboarding
New hires complete a 2-week onboarding program. In week one they finish security training, set up their laptop and meet their onboarding buddy. In week two they shadow their team and complete their first starter task. Every new hire has a 30-60-90 day check-in with their manager.

## Learning Budget
Each employee has an annual learning budget of 1,500 USD that can be spent on courses, certifications, books and conferences. Requests are submitted through PeopleHub.
