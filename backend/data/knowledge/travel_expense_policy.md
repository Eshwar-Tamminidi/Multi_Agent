# Nimbus Corp Travel and Expense Policy

## Booking Travel
All business travel must be booked through the TravelDesk portal. Domestic flights must be booked in economy class. International flights longer than 8 hours may be booked in premium economy. Trips must be approved by the employee's manager before booking.

## Daily Allowances
The daily meal allowance is 60 USD for domestic travel and 90 USD for international travel. Hotel stays are capped at 180 USD per night in standard cities and 260 USD per night in high-cost cities such as New York, London, Tokyo and Singapore.

## Submitting Expenses
Expense claims must be submitted in the ExpenseFlow app within 30 days of the expense being incurred. Itemised receipts are required for any single expense above 25 USD. Claims submitted after 90 days will not be reimbursed. Approved claims are reimbursed in the next payroll cycle.

## Non-Reimbursable Items
Alcohol, personal entertainment, traffic fines, minibar charges and upgrades purchased for personal preference are not reimbursable.

## Corporate Cards
Employees who travel more than 4 times per year may request a corporate card. Corporate card statements must be reconciled in ExpenseFlow by the 5th of every month.
