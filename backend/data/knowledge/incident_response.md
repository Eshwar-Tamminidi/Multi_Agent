# Nimbus Corp Incident Response Runbook

## Severity Levels
SEV1 is a complete outage of a customer-facing service or a confirmed data breach. SEV2 is a major degradation affecting more than 20% of customers. SEV3 is a minor issue with a workaround available. SEV4 is a cosmetic issue.

## Response Targets
For SEV1 incidents an incident commander must be assigned within 5 minutes and a status page update published within 15 minutes. SEV2 incidents require acknowledgement within 15 minutes. SEV3 incidents must be acknowledged within 4 business hours.

## On-Call Rotation
Each engineering team maintains a weekly on-call rotation that starts every Monday at 09:00. On-call engineers receive a stipend of 200 USD per week. Pages are routed through PagerDuty.

## Post-Incident Review
A blameless post-incident review must be written within 5 business days of every SEV1 and SEV2 incident. Reviews include a timeline, root cause analysis, and action items with owners and due dates.

## Communication
Customer communication for SEV1 incidents is owned by the Customer Success team, who send updates every 30 minutes until resolution.
